# Third-party notices

Slowpage for Android is MIT-licensed (`LICENSE`). The APK also carries the work of others, listed here. Every licence text below ships inside the app: About → Credits and licences.

Most of these are not stored in this repository. `scripts/build-rust.sh` takes them, unmodified, from the iPad app's repository at a pinned revision (`inkpad-ipados` at `873d4f4`), which in turn copied them from the Linux app's repository ([HushHour](https://github.com/50hoursaweek/hushhour)) at a tagged release.
